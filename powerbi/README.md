What the Dashboard Shows

The dashboard focuses on five areas:

  Financial Performance: Total invoiced revenue, invoice count, average invoice value, and billed customers.

  Monthly Trends: Changes in invoiced revenue over time.
  
  Subscription Plans: Comparison of invoiced revenue across different plans.

  Customer Segments: Financial performance by industry and region.

  Top Customers: The 10 customers contributing the most invoiced revenue.

  Interactive filters allow users to explore the data by date, subscription plan, industry, and region.

Data Modeling

The report uses a star schema developed in MySQL.

I also created DAX measures to calculate financial KPIs and analyze month-over-month changes.

Financial Note

The dashboard measures invoiced revenue, meaning amounts billed to customers. This is different from cash collected or recognized accounting revenue.

Data Disclaimer

All data is synthetic and represents a fictional SaaS company.
